A FEW QUICK STEPS

Save BandPlan to your device

Choose your device below. You only need to do this once.

iPhone / iPadAndroidComputer

SAFARI

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open BandPlan in Safari.

    Go to app.bandplan.app.

  2. Tap Share.

    Look for the square with an arrow pointing up. You may need to open the browser menu first.

  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.

    Scroll down the list of options to find it.

  4. Tap Add.

    If you see Open as Web App, turn it on before adding.

You’re set! Tap the BandPlan icon on your home screen to open it.

CHROME

Android

  1. Open BandPlan in Chrome.

    Go to app.bandplan.app.

  2. Open the menu.

    Tap the three dots near the address bar.

  3. Find the install option.

    Look for Add to Home screen, Install app, or Install and create shortcut.

  4. Confirm to finish.

    Tap Install or Add and follow any prompts.

You’re set! Look for BandPlan on your home screen or in your app list.

Opened this from Facebook or an email?

If you can’t find these options, open app.bandplan.app directly in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android, then follow the steps above. Menu names can vary by device and browser version.

ON A BIGGER SCREEN

Using a computer?

Visit app.bandplan.app in your browser and log in. Bookmark it to find it easily next time.
