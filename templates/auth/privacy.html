BandPlan Privacy Policy

Effective July 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information BandPlan collects, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Name and display name
  • Email address
  • Password hash
  • Profile picture

Band content

  • Song information
  • Setlists
  • Notes and chat messages
  • Uploaded images, audio, charts, and documents

Technical information

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Session cookies
  • Server logs and error reports

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Operate and maintain BandPlan.
  • Authenticate users and secure accounts.
  • Store and display band content.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Improve performance and reliability.
  • Prevent abuse and unauthorized access.

3. Cookies

BandPlan uses cookies to keep you signed in, maintain secure sessions, and support normal operation of the service.

4. Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information:

  • With service providers that help host or operate BandPlan.
  • When required by law.
  • When reasonably necessary to protect BandPlan, its users, or the public.

5. Third-Party Services

BandPlan may connect with third-party music, storage, calendar, analytics, or hosting services. Those services maintain their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no internet service or storage system is completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide BandPlan, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and personal information by contacting us. Shared band content may need to be handled separately when it belongs to or is used by other band members.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or request a copy of your personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

BandPlan is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as BandPlan evolves. When changes are made, we will update the effective date.

12. Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to support@bandplan.app .
